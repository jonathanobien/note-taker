1 · What is cloud computing?

Cloud computing lets you access, work on, share, and store information over the internet instead of only on your own computer. The manual nicknames it "Everything as a Service" (XaaS). Behind the scenes, the "cloud" is a network of huge data centers — thousands of computers working together — that deliver applications and services on demand.

In plain terms: Instead of owning and maintaining your own computer power, you "plug into" someone else's — like getting electricity from the grid rather than running your own generator.

2 · The three types of cloud deployment

TypeWhat it isEveryday example
Private
(Internal)		Used by one organization only; data sits behind its own firewall.A bank running its own secure internal systems.
Public
(External)		Open to the public on a provider's shared servers; pay-per-use, no hardware to buy.Gmail, or services on Huawei Cloud / AWS.
HybridA mix of private and public — keep sensitive parts private, put the rest on public cloud.A store keeping customer data private but using public cloud for its website.

FIT Academy deploys SAP Business One on Huawei Cloud for its school partners.

4 · What is SAP?

SAP = Systems, Applications, and Products in data processing — a German multinational founded in 1972 in Walldorf, Germany, by five former IBM engineers. It commands about 67% of the business-software market.

5 · What is SAP Business One?

SAP Business One is an ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) solution organized into 15 functional modules. Its four selling points: Integrated, Real-time, Flexible, and Easy to use.

Beyond the manual — what "ERP" really means

ERP software connects a company's main departments — finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, production — into one system with one shared database. Enter data once, and it's instantly correct everywhere.

6 · On-Premise vs On-Cloud

MeasureOn-PremiseOn-Cloud
DeploymentInstalled on-site at the companyDelivered through the internet
AccessibilityLimited to the premisesRemote access from anywhere
Data StorageLocalized / independentOn the cloud server
AccountingLocal SAP installationBranch accounting
In plain terms: On-premise is like owning a car; on-cloud is like ride-hailing — reached through the internet, usable anywhere, maintained by someone else.
