Chapter 1

SAP on Cloud Platform

Learning objectives

  1. Describe the different types of cloud deployment.
  2. Identify the key features of cloud computing.
  3. Differentiate on-premise vs on-cloud SAP Business One.

1 · What is cloud computing?

Cloud computing lets you access, work on, share, and store information over the internet instead of only on your own computer. The manual nicknames it "Everything as a Service" (XaaS). Behind the scenes, the "cloud" is a network of huge data centers — thousands of computers working together — that deliver applications and services on demand.

In plain terms: Instead of owning and maintaining your own computer power, you "plug into" someone else's — like getting electricity from the grid rather than running your own generator. You use what you need and the provider keeps the machines running.

2 · The three types of cloud deployment

TypeWhat it isEveryday example
Private
(Internal)		Used by one organization only; data sits behind its own firewall. Hosted on-site or by a third party, but reserved for that company.A bank running its own secure internal systems.
Public
(External)		Open to the public on a provider's shared servers. No hardware for you to buy; usually pay-per-use or licensed.Gmail, or services on AWS / Huawei Cloud.
HybridA mix of private and public — keep sensitive parts private, put the rest on public cloud.A store keeping customer data private but using public cloud for its website.

FIT Academy deploys SAP Business One on Huawei Cloud for its school partners — a platform offering 200+ cloud services and a large partner marketplace.

4 · What is SAP?

SAP stands for Systems, Applications, and Products in data processing — a German multinational founded in 1972 in Walldorf, Germany, by five former IBM engineers. It's one of the world's largest business-software companies, commanding about 67% of the business-software market, with offices in 180 countries.

5 · What is SAP Business One?

SAP Business One is an ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) solution organized into 15 functional modules that automate the major functions of a business. Its four selling points: it is Integrated, Real-time, Flexible, and Easy to use.

Beyond the manual — what "ERP" really means

ERP software connects a company's main departments — finance, sales, purchasing, inventory, production — into one system with one shared database. So when a sale is recorded, inventory and accounting update automatically. "Integrated + real-time" is exactly this: enter data once, and it's instantly correct everywhere.

6 · On-Premise vs On-Cloud

MeasureOn-PremiseOn-Cloud
DeploymentInstalled on-site at the companyDelivered through the internet
AccessibilityLimited to the installation's premisesRemote access from anywhere
Data StorageLocalized / independentOn the cloud server
AccountingLocal SAP installationBranch accounting
In plain terms: On-premise is like owning a car — you buy it, park it, and maintain it yourself. On-cloud is like ride-hailing — you reach it through the internet, go anywhere, and someone else maintains the vehicle.

Chapter 2

Getting Started

Learning objectives

  1. Log in to SAP Business One.
  2. Identify the parts of the user interface and basic navigation tools.

1 · How to log in

  1. Open any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
  2. Type the URL from your instructor in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. The SAP Business One log-in page appears.
  4. Select the company your instructor provides.
  5. Log in with the User ID & Password for your assigned branch.

📱 On a phone, turn on “Desktop Site” so the interface displays properly.

Beyond the manual — why branches?

In a class, everyone shares one company file, so each student gets a numbered branch (Branch 1–42). Your branch keeps your work separate from your classmates'. Logging into the wrong branch means working in someone else's data — always use the one assigned to you.

2 · The user interface

PartWhereWhat it does
Menu BarTop of screenWindows menus (File, Edit, Window, Help) plus general SAP functions.
Tool BarUnder the menu barIcon buttons for commonly-used functions — shortcuts to things also found in the menu bar.
Modules MenuMain navigation panelLists all modules and their options in a tree structure.

Three facts about the Modules Menu

  • Arranged in the same order as the Main Menu.
  • Cannot be modified.
  • May be inactive for unauthorized users (you only see what you're allowed to).

3 · Change your user name & password

From the toolbar, open My Personal Settings (the User Set-up window appears):

  1. Change the User Name field to your own name.
  2. Click beside the Password field to personalize it.
  3. Enter the old password, then your new password in the Password and Confirm fields.
  4. Click OK, then Update.

4 · Set your branch name

Navigate to:

Administration▸Setup▸Financials▸Branches

  1. In the Branches Setup window, find your assigned branch.
  2. Change Branch Name and Branch Name (Foreign) to your own name.
  3. Click Update.

5 · Basic customization (display settings)

Set how things display:

Administration▸System Initialization▸General Settings

On the Display tab, set:

SettingValue
LanguageEnglish (United States)
Time Format12H
Date FormatMM/DD/YYYY

Then click Update and OK.

🔎 Font size/style can’t be changed inside SAP — increase your browser’s zoom % instead.
